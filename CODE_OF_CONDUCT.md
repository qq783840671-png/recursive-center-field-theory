# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository welcomes careful criticism, counterexamples, reproductions, documentation improvements, and implementation work. Participants are expected to make the project safer to examine and easier to falsify, not to protect the theory or its author from criticism.

We are committed to a community in which people can participate without harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attack, regardless of background, identity, experience, viewpoint, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, definitions, evidence, code, and documented behavior rather than attacking people.
- State whether a contribution concerns a definition, hypothesis, open question, implementation, or empirical result.
- Provide the smallest relevant quotation, address, version, example, or reproduction when making a strong claim.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and revise a claim when contrary evidence is established.
- Protect private conversations, credentials, unpublished manuscripts, personal data, and security-sensitive details.
- Allow maintainers to redirect broad discussion away from issue trackers intended for actionable work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, or sustained disruption.
- Presenting disagreement with the theory as a judgment about a participant's intelligence or character.
- Fabricating evidence, impersonating others, or knowingly misrepresenting sources or test results.
- Publishing private or security-sensitive material without authorization.
- Repeatedly reopening a settled moderation decision without new evidence.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, reject, or remove contributions; issue warnings; or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project record.

For ordinary, non-sensitive moderation requests, open an issue and explain the affected repository content without repeating harmful material. For threats, doxxing, or other sensitive abuse, use GitHub's private content-reporting controls or [GitHub's abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not publish sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when safe, and separated from judgments about whether a theoretical claim is true.

## Attribution

This is a project-specific conduct policy informed by common open collaboration practices and GitHub's community moderation guidance. It is not a verbatim copy of the Contributor Covenant.
