# References and Citation Boundaries

**Artifact:** source-available `v0.4.0-alpha.2` · **Root theory:** `v0.13` · **Dynamics:** `v0.18` · **Multi-center specification:** `v0.7-draft`

This is a consolidated reading and citation map for the public draft. It records sources already used in the canonical theory and comparison documents. It is not a systematic literature review, patent search, or proof of novelty.

## How to read this list

- A citation can establish what another author or method says; it does not establish that Recursive Center-Field Theory (RCF) is new or empirically effective.
- “Structural proximity” means that two accounts occupy comparable functional positions. It does not mean identity, direct inheritance, synthesis, or superiority.
- Publisher pages, workshop pages, standards, engineering reports, and primary papers carry different evidential force. Their role is marked below.
- References are linked for access. A later scholarly edition should normalize authors, translators, editions, page numbers, and archival identifiers.

## 1. Philosophical lineage and non-identity

These sources support the draft's working position as a **post-foundationally oriented process–functional structural account**. None contains the complete RCF protocol.

### Problem situations, process, and relative stability

- John Dewey, *Logic: The Theory of Inquiry*, “The Pattern of Inquiry.” [Accessible text](https://archive.org/stream/JohnDeweyLogicTheTheoryOfInquiry/%5BJohn_Dewey%5D_Logic_-_The_Theory_of_Inquiry_djvu.txt). Used for the movement from an indeterminate situation through inquiry toward a provisionally determinate one. **Boundary:** no candidate-center set, recursive address system, Focus motion, or modal residual ledger.
- Gilbert Simondon, “The Position of the Problem of Ontogenesis.” [Parrhesia translation](https://parrhesiajournal.org/parrhesia07/parrhesia07_simondon1.pdf). Used for metastability, individuation, and the persistence of unresolved potential. **Boundary:** the preindividual is not a database omission or merely unclassified information.
- Niklas Luhmann, “Operational Closure and Structural Coupling: The Differentiation of the Legal System.” [Cardozo Law Review](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/clr/vol13/iss5/2/). Used for the relation among operational closure, boundary formation, and environment coupling. **Boundary:** Luhmann's communication systems are not user-redrawn task fields.

### Determinate transformation, center, and temporary grounding

- G. W. F. Hegel, *Phenomenology of Spirit*, §79. [Cambridge excerpt](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/55792/excerpt/9780521855792_excerpt.pdf).
- G. W. F. Hegel, *Encyclopaedia of the Philosophical Sciences*, §96 Zusatz. [Online German text](https://hegel.de/werke_frei/hw108017.htm). These are used for determinate negation and the cancellation/preservation double meaning of *Aufheben*. **Boundary:** dialectical sublation is not technical version control, and RCF does not presuppose an absolute final center or necessary historical completion.
- Jacques Derrida, “Structure, Sign, and Play in the Discourse of the Human Sciences,” in *Writing and Difference*, translated by Alan Bass. [University of Chicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/W/bo27619783.html). Used for the center's organizing and limiting function and for the de-reification of center. **Boundary:** RCF adds a task-relative operational protocol; this is not an algorithm supplied by Derrida.
- Oliver Marchart, *Post-Foundational Political Thought*. [Publisher page](https://edinburghuniversitypress.com/book-post-foundational-political-thought.html). Used for the distinction between the absence of an ultimate ground and the continuing activity of contingent grounding. **Boundary:** political grounding is not identical to a functional center or partial-order task model.

### Anti-reification constraints

- Jacques Lacan, *The Seminar of Jacques Lacan, Book XI: The Four Fundamental Concepts of Psychoanalysis*, text established by Jacques-Alain Miller, translated by Alan Sheridan, ch. 5, “Tuché and Automaton,” pp. 53–66. [Publisher page](https://www.routledge.com/The-Four-Fundamental-Concepts-of-Psycho-Analysis/Lacan-Miller/p/book/9781855753570). Used as a warning that the limit of symbolization is not merely information waiting for a longer address. **Boundary:** the Real is not identical to an RCF residual or `[∅]` state; the publisher page supplies bibliographic access, while the chapter and pages identify the supporting passage.
- *Zhuangzi*, “Adjustment of Controversies” / “Qiwulun,” especially the passages containing “彼亦一是非，此亦一是非” and “道枢.” [Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/adjustment-of-controversies). Used to unsettle fixed this/that and right/wrong positions while retaining responsive differentiation. **Boundary:** it does not supply recursive state schemas or execution semantics.
- Nāgārjuna, *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*, especially 24.18 for dependent arising, emptiness, dependent designation, and the middle way, and 13.8 for the warning against turning emptiness itself into a fixed view. [GRETIL Sanskrit text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_nAgArjuna-mUlamadhyamakakArikA.htm); Mark Siderits and Shōryū Katsura, *Nāgārjuna's Middle Way*, [publisher page](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Nagarjunas-Middle-Way/Mark-Siderits/Classics-of-Indian-Buddhism/9781614290506). Used to constrain the reification of a field or center as self-existing. **Boundary:** task-relative center construction is not a restatement of emptiness.

The fuller argument and explicit non-identities are in [Philosophical lineage and non-identity](philosophical-lineage.zh-CN.md).

## 2. Knowledge representation, planning, and systems methods

These references establish strong prior art for hierarchy, heterogeneous relations, partial-order execution, recursion, interfaces, versions, and change control. RCF must not claim any of these components individually as new.

### Taxonomy, ontology, and graph representation

- W3C, [SKOS Simple Knowledge Organization System Reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/).
- W3C, [OWL 2 Web Ontology Language Primer](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-primer/).
- W3C, [OWL 2 Structural Specification and Functional-Style Syntax](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl-syntax/).
- W3C, [RDF 1.2 Concepts and Abstract Syntax](https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf12-concepts/).

These establish classes, broader/narrower relations, multiple hierarchies, typed semantics, graph representation, and open-world assumptions. **RCF distinction under test:** task contracts, candidate load-bearing centers, necessary-predecessor projection, modal field addresses, and residual-driven version motion.

### Workflows, Petri nets, and hierarchical planning

- Common Workflow Language, [Workflow specification 1.2](https://www.commonwl.org/v1.2/Workflow.html).
- ISO/IEC, [15909-1:2019 — High-level Petri Nets](https://www.iso.org/standard/67235.html).
- Tadao Murata, “Petri Nets: Properties, Analysis and Applications.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/5.24143).
- Kutluhan Erol, James Hendler, and Dana Nau, “HTN Planning: Complexity and Expressivity.” [AAAI paper](https://aaai.org/papers/01123-aaai94-173-htn-planning-complexity-and-expressivity/).
- Dana Nau et al., “SHOP2: An HTN Planning System.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1613/JAIR.1141).

These establish recursive task refinement, partial orders, enabling, concurrency, conflict, reachability, execution state, and recovery in established methods. **RCF distinction under test:** forming and auditing a provisional task-relative center, separating Global Expansion from unequal-depth Focus, and using residual evidence to revise the modeled field.

### Causality, modularity, and systems engineering

- Judea Pearl, “Causal Diagrams for Empirical Research.” [Author-hosted PDF](https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/R218-B.pdf).
- David Parnas, “On the Criteria To Be Used in Decomposing Systems into Modules.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/361598.361623).
- NASA, [System Design Processes](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-0-system-design-processes/).
- NASA, [Configuration Management](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-5-configuration-management/).

These establish causal identification limits, information-hiding criteria, recursive system decomposition, interfaces, baselines, traceability, and change management. **Boundary:** functional precedence is not automatically causal identification, and a Focus address is not evidence of a real-world causal mechanism.

- Shunyu Yao et al., “ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models.” [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629).
- Charles Packer et al., “MemGPT: Towards LLMs as Operating Systems.” [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560).
- Nelson Liu et al., “Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts.” [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172).
- Anthropic, [Effective Context Engineering for AI Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents).
- Anthropic, [Effective Harnesses for Long-Running Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents).
- [Agent Skills Specification](https://agentskills.io/specification).
- ICLR 2026 Workshop, [Memory for LLM-Based Agentic Systems](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/workshop/10000792).

These sources establish retrieval-augmented generation, dense retrieval, graph-based local/global query strategies, reasoning–action loops, memory architectures, long-context failure modes, context-engineering practices, long-running-agent harnesses, and the Skill packaging format.

**RCF positioning:** retrieval or graph methods produce candidate material; Focus is proposed as a task-relative structure-compilation and context-orchestration layer above them. This has not been shown to improve retrieval or agent performance.

**Evidence cautions:**

- The Anthropic items are first-party engineering reports, not general controlled evidence for all agents.
- The Agent Skills document specifies a packaging interface; using that interface is not theoretical novelty.
- The ICLR workshop page shows that agent memory is an active topic. A workshop listing is not conference consensus or validation of Focus.

## 4. Reinforcement learning and explicit policy structure

- Richard Sutton, Doina Precup, and Satinder Singh, “Between MDPs and Semi-MDPs: A Framework for Temporal Abstraction in Reinforcement Learning.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(99)00052-1).
- Ronald Parr and Stuart Russell, “Reinforcement Learning with Hierarchies of Machines.” [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/1997/hash/5ca3e9b122f61f8f06494c97b1afccf3-Abstract.html).
- Pierre-Luc Bacon, Jean Harb, and Doina Precup, “The Option-Critic Architecture.” [AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/10916).
- Shengyi Huang and Santiago Ontañón, “A Closer Look at Invalid Action Masking in Policy Gradient Algorithms.” [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14171).
- Michele Colledanchise and Petter Ögren, *Behavior Trees in Robotics and AI*. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00084).

These sources establish temporal abstraction, hierarchical machines, learned options, action masking, and interpretable modular control. **RCF must not claim** that reducing a search space, introducing hierarchy, masking illegal actions, or exposing a control route is new.

The narrower hypothesis is that a task-relative, evidence-audited partial-order protocol may supply legality, address, invalidation, and residual semantics around a learned policy. It must be compared directly with native masks, hierarchical RL, and modular control; see [Evaluation](evaluation.md).

## 5. What the references do and do not support

| Statement | Current support |
|---|---|
| The philosophical problem has historical neighbors | Supported as a structured dialogue, with explicit non-identity |
| Recursive hierarchy, partial orders, graphs, interfaces, and versioning have extensive prior art | Strongly supported |
| Long-context state recovery and agent memory are active engineering/research problems | Supported, but the cited evidence is heterogeneous |
| The exact RCF combination is historically novel | **Not established**; requires systematic literature, patent, and implementation review |
| Focus improves retrieval, drift, context recovery, or RL | **Not established**; requires controlled studies |
| The RCF pattern is a universal law of reality | **Not established**; remains a strong ontological hypothesis |

## 6. Known citation and prior-art gaps

The following gaps should remain visible in the public draft:

1. A systematic prior-art search for the full combination—task-relative contract, candidate-center formation and audit, necessary partial order, dual expansion, reopenable compression, audited write-back, and residual-driven reconstruction—has not been completed.
2. The philosophy section still needs edition- and page-stable citations, especially where an accessible excerpt or publisher page currently substitutes for a full scholarly edition.
3. Kant, phenomenology, structuralism, Foucault, and Deleuze have not yet received author-and-text-level comparisons.
4. Non-European traditions need deeper treatment that does not instrumentalize them as confirmations of an externally imposed framework.
5. Several production agent-memory baselines still need primary or authoritative technical citations.
6. “Context engineering” is currently supported mainly by practitioner documentation rather than one stable, field-wide definition.
7. No public benchmark yet validates drift reduction, context recovery, or RL benefits under matched total cost.
8. No citation can substitute for an operational account of center existence, candidate disagreement, or analyst reproducibility.

## 7. Citation practice for this repository

Use the repository's [`CITATION.cff`](../CITATION.cff) to cite RCF, its protocol, or the bundled Skill. Keep its release version, author identity, repository URL, and release date synchronized with the published artifact.

When citing an external source in a claim:

- place the citation next to the exact supported statement;
- identify whether it is a primary text, standard, research paper, engineering report, publisher page, or workshop page;
- state the non-identity when drawing a philosophical or structural analogy;
- do not cite a related method as evidence that Focus outperforms it;
- do not infer novelty from the absence of a result in this non-systematic list.

This reference map should be updated by adding verifiable sources and narrowing claims, not by converting unresolved gaps into rhetorical certainty.
